Import Old Data via CSV

Paste a CSV with your existing bonuses. Only bank and bonus_amount are required. All other columns are optional — leave them blank and the app fills in defaults.

Export your Google Sheet as CSV, paste below.
Expected columns (your exact headers, in any order):
bank, person, type, doc link, avoid fee, open date,
expected bonus 2026, last inquiry date, bonus req,
req met, req missing, next step, bonus date, close,
anticipate d/earliest, addition comment, tax

Tips: Leave merged bank cells as-is (blank rows carry forward the last bank). Person names like Ishan → You, Heli → Spouse are auto-mapped. Dates like 03/08/2024 and amounts like 50k-06/30/2024 or avg $500 are auto-parsed.
Standard column names (any order, header row required):
bank, account_name, account_last4, person, status, bonus_amount,
date_opened, date_closed, date_bonus_posted, deadline, cooldown_months,
dd_amount, dd_count, min_balance, monthly_fee, etf_amount, etf_days,
